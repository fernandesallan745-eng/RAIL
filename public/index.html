ℹ️ Dispatch & Precedence Assumptions ▾
Crossing geometry is computed from the other trains' scheduled times (static) against this train's live delay — we do not poll other trains. Loop locations are assumed at every timetable station on the single-line section; this source carries no track-count data. Precedence is a heuristic over train type, not official Indian Railways rules — the real call is a Section Controller's. Prediction only; no dispatch action is issued.

ℹ️ How a report is scored ▾
Confidence is a weighted heuristic over five signals — how near the pin is to the alignment, whether a photo or description was supplied, how many independent devices reported the same thing nearby in time, the reporter's own history, and whether a train was due through. It is not a trained or validated model: there is no ground-truth hazard set to calibrate against. It can raise a report to corroborated at most. Only a human controller confirms a report, and only a confirmed report is allowed to affect an ETA. Speed caps are our heuristic, not sourced TSR values.
